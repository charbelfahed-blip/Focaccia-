Extra olive oil: for pans and dimpling, plus rosemary and flaky salt. Grease the pans generously for a crisp base.

Yeast quantities assume instant dry yeast and a refrigerator near 4°C. If using active dry or fresh yeast, convert separately. A precise scale is useful for small yeast amounts.

02 / Plan your bake

A timeline that works

Set when you plan to begin mixing.

Timeline estimate: allow 1 hour 30 minutes for mixing and folds, then 24 or 48 hours in the fridge, then around 3 hours to proof in pans. Bake when the dough is puffy and jiggly, not strictly by the clock.

Baker's notes

For a good 3–4 cm rise

Give it room to puff. Don't forcefully stretch or deflate the dough after the cold ferment.
Use a metal pan. Avoid glass if the goal is a really crisp, golden base.
Preheat fully. Bake in your home oven at 230°C / 445°F, typically 25–35 minutes. Use a lower rack if the bottom needs more colour.
Lift it out promptly. Cool on a wire rack, so the underside doesn't steam and soften.
